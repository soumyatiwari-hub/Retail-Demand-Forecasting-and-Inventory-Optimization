# Week 3 Report

## Project Title

Retail Demand Forecasting & Inventory Optimization

# Objective

The objective of Week 3 was to transform the analytical results into interactive Tableau dashboards for business users. The dashboards were designed to visualize sales trends, product performance, and business insights, enabling easier decision-making through interactive visualizations.

---

# Tasks Completed

### 1. Connected Dataset to Tableau

- Imported the prepared dataset into Tableau Public.
- Verified data types and field names.
- Connected the dataset successfully for visualization.

---

### 2. Dashboard 1: Retail Demand Forecasting Dashboard

Created an interactive dashboard containing:

- Daily Sales Trend
- Store-wise Sales Performance
- Category-wise Sales Analysis

Purpose:

- Monitor sales trends over time.
- Compare sales across stores.
- Analyze sales by product category.

---

### 3. Dashboard 2: Business Insights Dashboard

Created a second dashboard containing:

- Monthly Sales Trend
- Average Selling Price
- Top 10 Products

Purpose:

- Identify monthly sales patterns.
- Compare average selling prices.
- Identify the highest-performing products.
